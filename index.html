About Addu Dive Hub

YOUR GATEWAY TO MALDIVES' SOUTHERN FRONTIER

Addu Dive Hub is situated in the pristine Addu Atoll, the southernmost tip of the Maldives. Unlike northern atolls, Addu was unaffected by coral bleaching, preserving lush hard corals and vibrant biodiversity.

Our local dive guides and PADI Instructors carry decades of local expertise, offering safe, eco-conscious, and unforgettable underwater journeys.
